# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：September 21, 2026 / 2026 年 9 月 21 日

## English

MenuTrim works locally on your Mac. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, account system, or developer-operated network service.

MenuTrim stores preferences such as launch behavior, automatic hiding delay, and compatible menu bar spacing choices on your Mac. It does not send these preferences to the developer.

To hide selected third-party menu bar icons, MenuTrim requests macOS Device Control and Data Access permission. When you choose Hide, it reads the positions and owning application identifiers of visible third-party menu bar items to determine which apps are located to the left of the MenuTrim divider. This information is processed locally and is not uploaded or retained as usage history.

MenuTrim does not collect personal data and does not track you across apps or websites.

## 中文

MenuTrim 全程在你的 Mac 本机运行，不包含广告、统计、跟踪、第三方 SDK、账户系统或开发者运营的网络服务。

MenuTrim 会在本机保存登录启动、自动隐藏延时和兼容菜单栏项目间距等设置，不会将这些设置发送给开发者。

为隐藏指定的第三方菜单栏图标，MenuTrim 需要 macOS“设备控制与数据访问”权限。执行隐藏操作时，它会读取当前可见第三方菜单栏项目的位置和所属应用标识，以判断哪些应用位于 MenuTrim 分界线左侧。相关信息仅在本机处理，不上传，也不作为使用记录保存。

MenuTrim 不收集个人数据，也不会跨应用或网站跟踪用户。
