# Changelog / 更新记录

## 1.0.3 — 2026-11-22

- Added a separate MenuTrim clock with customizable time, date, weekday, and time zone.
- The arrow and clock can be positioned independently. The macOS clock can be hidden or restored independently of the app icons.
- 新增独立 MenuTrim 时钟，可自定义时间、日期、星期和时区。
- 箭头和时钟可以分别摆放，系统时钟可独立于应用图标隐藏或恢复。

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-23

- Hidden icons now stay hidden after switching desktops (Spaces), waking from sleep, unlocking the screen, or changing displays. Previously they could reappear while the chevron still showed the collapsed state, so the next click seemed to do nothing.
- Apps launched while icons are hidden are no longer hidden when their icons sit to the right of the chevron.
- Clicking the chevron right after launch is no longer overridden by the start-collapsed option.
- 切换桌面（空间）、从睡眠唤醒、解锁屏幕或更改显示器后，已隐藏的图标会保持隐藏。此前图标可能重新出现，而箭头仍显示为收起状态，导致下一次点击看似无效。
- 隐藏期间新启动的应用，若其图标位于箭头右侧，不会再被误隐藏。
- 启动后立即手动点击箭头，不会再被"启动时收起"选项覆盖。

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-22

- First stable MenuTrim release.
- Settings now show Version 1.0.0, author Yang Han (Hany), and the copyright notice.
- App and DMG are signed with Developer ID and distributed in an Apple-notarized, stapled DMG.
- MenuTrim 首个稳定版本。
- 设置窗口现显示 Version 1.0.0、作者 Yang Han (Hany) 及版权信息。
- 应用与 DMG 均已使用 Developer ID 签名，DMG 已通过 Apple 公证并写入公证票据。

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-21

- Added a native macOS 27 Icon Composer app icon.
- Distributed as a Developer ID signed app in an Apple notarized and stapled DMG.
- Kept application behavior unchanged.
- 新增 macOS 27 原生 Icon Composer 应用图标。
- 应用使用 Developer ID 签名，DMG 已通过 Apple 公证并写入公证票据。
- 应用功能保持不变。

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-21

- Initial public freeware release.
- Added one-click collapse and expand, auto collapse, keyboard shortcut, launch at login, multiple-display state handling, and spacing controls for compatible third-party items.
- 首个公开免费版本。
- 支持一键收起与展开、自动收起、键盘快捷键、登录启动、多显示器状态保持，以及兼容第三方图标的间距设置。
